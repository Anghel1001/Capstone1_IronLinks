About Us

IronLinks is a modern platform that connects customers with skilled local iron workers. We combine traditional craftsmanship with new technology to make custom iron designs easier to plan, view, and order online.

Our goal is to help small ironwork businesses grow through digital innovation while promoting sustainable and smart city development.

📍 Service Location

IronLinks is based in Apugan, Irisan, Baguio City. We proudly serve customers within Baguio City and nearby areas. Our team is also available for projects in surrounding municipalities depending on project size and scheduling availability.

Nearby service areas include:

  • La Trinidad, Benguet
  • Itogon, Benguet
  • Tuba, Benguet
  • Sablan, Benguet
  • Selected nearby Cordillera areas (by request)

For locations outside these areas, consultation and transport arrangements may be discussed during booking.
